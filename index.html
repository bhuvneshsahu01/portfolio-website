01

VeriCall — Execution-Verified Post-Training for Tool Calling

Post-trained Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct for REST tool calling with QLoRA SFT, then DPO on 3,200 execution-mined near-miss pairs from a 60-endpoint FastAPI sandbox (36 training endpoints, 24 held-out endpoints from 4 unseen APIs). On 500 requests across the 24 held-out endpoints, execution accuracy rose from 54.2% (base) and 77.6% (SFT) to 86.4% (95% Wilson CI [83.1%, 89.1%]; paired McNemar p < 0.001 vs. SFT), ahead of base + constrained decoding (68.4%). Slashed requests with a hallucinated parameter by -67% vs. SFT (58 to 19 of 500) and raised BFCL multiple-function AST accuracy (N=200) from 69.5% to 82.5%. A follow-up online GRPO run (binary execution reward, G=8, β=0.04) lifted Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct from 61.6% (SFT) to 74.8%. Engineered a completion-sliced PyTorch DPO trainer with precomputed reference log-probabilities, reducing logit memory from 1.83 GB to 18 MB (-99%) and peak VRAM to 6.8 GB on a 16 GB T4 (standard TRL ran out of memory). Served the AWQ-quantized merged model on vLLM (86.0% accuracy retained) at 640 ms p50 end-to-end and 14 req/s on one T4 with a fixed, prefix-cached tool set (85 ms p50 TTFT; 380 ms uncached).

TraceGuard (traceguard-ai) — LLM Drift Monitoring & Guardrails SDK

Published traceguard-ai on PyPI (Pytest, GitHub Actions CI) for LLM traffic drift detection: PSI, KS, and χ² checks on scalar and categorical signals plus permutation-tested kernel MMD on prompt/response embeddings with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR control across p-value tests; measured a 3.8% false-alarm rate under H0 (α=0.05) and 89.6% power on a 25% out-of-domain semantic shift. Implemented async FastAPI guardrail middleware (regex/Luhn and Presidio pattern-based PII redaction, TF-IDF + heuristic prompt-injection screening, token budgets, OpenTelemetry spans) adding 3.0 ms p95 per 128-token prompt; caught 76.7% (46/60) of injections on deepset/prompt-injections with 1 false positive in 56 benign prompts (1.8% FPR), and 71.2% (712/1,000) out of distribution on Lakera/gandalf_ignore_instructions.

03

Rankwise — Two-Stage Recommender with Counterfactual Evaluation

Trained a two-stage recommender on KuaiRand-1K (250k+ videos after filtering): a Two-Tower retriever (in-batch softmax with log-Q correction, FAISS HNSW) feeding a multi-task DCN-v2 ranker that raised NDCG@10 on held-out unbiased random-exposure traffic from 0.412 (LightGBM LambdaMART) to 0.448 (paired bootstrap 95% CI [+0.024, +0.048], p < 0.001). Evaluated off-policy estimators against that unbiased ground truth: naive estimated-propensity IPS overstated policy value 2.1× (0.384 vs. 0.182), while propensity-clipped SNIPS landed within 6.4% relative error; served the stack behind FastAPI (log-replayed Redis store, ONNX Runtime, isotonic calibration lowering ECE from 0.068 to 0.014) at 11.8 ms p95 (24.5 ms p99) and 320 RPS on an 8-core CPU over a 5-minute load test.
