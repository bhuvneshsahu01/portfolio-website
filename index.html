Engineering Philosophy

Evaluation-First ML & Hardware-Aware Systems

Trained in mathematical statistics at Chennai Mathematical Institute (CMI) and Hindu College, University of Delhi, I approach machine learning systems from the evaluation harness backward. Before optimizing model architecture, I focus on eliminating data leakage across temporal splits, establishing honest deterministic or GBDT baselines, calibrating output probabilities, and bounding uncertainty with paired non-parametric confidence intervals and multiple-testing corrections.

On the systems side, I care equally about what happens after a checkpoint is saved: profiling logit-buffer VRAM bottlenecks during preference optimization, exploiting prefix caching and 4-bit activation-aware quantization in serving engines, and keeping guardrail and ranking latencies within strict single-digit to low-double-digit millisecond SLAs on commodity hardware.
